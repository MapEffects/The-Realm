# The Realm

## Project Specification

### Vision

**The Realm is a living map of the customer journey, not just an analytics dashboard.**

It transforms business data into a visual representation of how people discover, explore, and engage with Map Effects.

Rather than overwhelming the user with charts and spreadsheets, The Realm tells the story of how travelers move through the world of Map Effects and where new opportunities can be found.

The map is not decoration.

**The map is the interface.**

---

## Purpose

The Realm exists to help steward and grow Map Effects by making it easy to understand what is thriving, what needs attention, and where people are naturally drawn.

Its purpose is not simply to measure business performance, but to reveal how the journey through Map Effects can continually improve so that more people discover the joy of creating maps and telling stories.

---

## Design Philosophy

Every design decision should follow these principles.

- Visual before verbal.
- Journeys before statistics.
- Maps before dashboards.
- Beauty encourages engagement.
- Simplicity over complexity.
- The homepage is sacred.
- Every feature must earn its place.

The health of the Realm should be understood in less than ten seconds.

---

## The Districts

The homepage of The Realm is the capital city.

Each district answers a single important question.

### 🛣 Roads

**Where are travelers entering the Realm?**

Roads represent traffic sources.

Examples:

- Pinterest Road
- Instagram Road
- YouTube Road
- Email Road
- Google Road
- Other Roads

The homepage displays only the primary roads.

Clicking Roads opens a detailed view with every traffic source.

---

### ⛺ Camp

**Who has joined the Realm?**

The Camp represents the email community.

It answers:

- Total subscribers
- New subscribers today
- New subscribers this week

---

### 📚 Scholars' Quarter

**What are people discovering?**

The Scholars' Quarter contains the Realm's landmarks.

A landmark may be:

- A Pinterest Pin
- A Blog Article
- A YouTube Tutorial
- A Map Tip
- An Email
- A Landing Page

The homepage displays only the top-performing landmarks.

Clicking the district reveals the complete archive.

---

### 🏪 Marketplace

**What are travelers buying?**

The Marketplace contains every product sold by Map Effects.

The homepage highlights:

- Top Sellers
- Rising Sellers

Clicking the Marketplace opens detailed product analytics.

---

### 🏰 Treasury

**Is the Realm prospering?**

The Treasury summarizes the overall health of the business.

Key metrics include:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Average Order Value

---

### 📜 Royal Chronicle

The Royal Chronicle is the steward's daily briefing.

It summarizes the most important developments since the previous visit and surfaces one or more meaningful questions worth investigating.

The Chronicle exists to answer one question:

**What deserves my attention today?**

---

## Guiding Principle

The Realm should not overwhelm.

It should clarify.

The homepage should answer important questions rather than expose every piece of data.

Every district is a doorway to deeper information.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The Realm will become the central operating system for Map Effects.

It will eventually connect marketing, products, customer journeys, content performance, email growth, and future Map Effects software into a single cohesive ecosystem.
